# Project Scope: AIIM Impact Innovation Hub

## Executive Summary
**AIIM Impact Innovation Hub** is an enterprise-grade, AI-native platform designed to bridge the gap between high-impact startups, angel investors, venture capital funds, and ecosystem partners. By integrating Google's Gemini Generative AI, the platform automates deal flow analysis, pitch deck valuation, negotiation room intelligence, and ecosystem mapping.

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Impact-focused startups in emerging markets face fragmented access to capital, mentorship, and enterprise deals. Simultaneously, investors waste hundreds of hours manually screening unstructured pitch decks, evaluating valuation models, and conducting preliminary due diligence.

---

## 2. Core Vision & Objectives
To build a Fortune 500-ready innovation marketplace that leverages AI to democratize fundraising, deal room negotiations, and venture building.

### Key Objectives:
- **Democratize Deal Flow:** Automate pitch deck analysis and founder-funder matching using Gemini AI.
- **Enterprise Deal Room:** Provide real-time negotiation, valuation modeling, and document management.
- **Ecosystem Integration:** Connect startups with corporate partners, university incubators, and mentors.
- **Zero-Friction UX:** Deliver a fast, responsive, PWA-enabled interface built with React 19, Vite, and Tailwind CSS.

---

## 3. Key Target Personas
1. **Startup Founders:** Looking for seed/series-A funding, pitch deck refinement, and strategic corporate partners.
2. **Investors & VCs:** Seeking curated deal flow, automated due diligence reports, and valuation sanity checks.
3. **Ecosystem Partners & Accelerators:** Hosting innovation challenges, co-working spaces, and corporate innovation programs.
4. **School/University Admins:** Managing student entrepreneurship hubs, research commercialization, and incubators.

---

## 4. Out of Scope (Non-Goals)
- Live fiat payment settlement (handled via sandbox payment simulation).
- Direct cap table legal filing (provides AI templates and analytical breakdowns rather than legal binding filings).
- On-premise self-hosted cluster deployment (designed as cloud-native SaaS).

---

## 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **Time-to-Match:** Reduced from 14 days to under 2 minutes using Gemini AI matching.
- **Due Diligence Efficiency:** 75% reduction in manual pitch evaluation time.
- **User Experience:** Sub-100ms UI response times with 100% lighthouse accessibility compliance.
